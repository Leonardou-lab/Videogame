Game Description

Survive the throne room siege

Protect a king who would rather run

The Coward King is a turn-based tactical card game where the player protects a frightened monarch through escalating hordes. Victory is not about clearing every enemy. It is about surviving long enough for the king to escape.

Cards, allies, walls, and desperate movement

Every turn asks for a choice: place allies, build defenses, trigger traps, preserve AP, or move the king before desperation overtakes him.

Three levels of pressure

Catacombs teach the siege, ogres turn the board into a war of endurance, and elite warriors force faster reactions before the Brave King arrives.

Tutorial

A field guide for a very nervous crown

01

The king must live

The Coward King stands at the center of a 3x3 safe zone. Enemies want to overrun that space. If enemy pressure inside the safe zone reaches 2, the defense collapses.

02

Spend AP like royal coin

The player starts with 5 AP and cannot hold more than 5. Cards cost AP every time they are used. If the king does not move during a turn, the next turn gains 1 AP.

03

Summon defenders

Allies such as Knight, Archer, and Wall hold the line. They block tiles, absorb attacks, and strike enemies based on their range.

04

Move before panic wins

If the king refuses to move, Desperation rises. Moving the king resets the meter. At 4 Desperation, the king surrenders to fear and the run is lost.

05

Survive, do not chase

A horde ends when its turn limit is survived. Remaining enemies vanish because the king escapes deeper into the level. After each horde, choose one card to keep for the next hand.

06

Face the rulers of each level

After three hordes, a boss fight begins. Bosses occupy 2x2 tiles, count as heavy safe-zone pressure, and must be defeated to clear the level.

Now put the lesson to the test. Play The Coward King
